Instruments

Run on the built site, the same files that are deployed.

Impeccable detector, in a real browser
10 routes at 1440 and 390 px: 0 findings on 8 of 10. On the not-sure and no-provider states it flags the seal (64 points and a hood) as an illustration built from shapes. It is the logo glyph, so it stays.
Taste-skill lint, on the source
0 failures. 19 lines carry a written exception: real table rows, floating overlays (menus, sheets, tooltips), the scrim under the hero headline, and monospace kept for hashes and keys.
Taste-skill inspector, presentation pages
0 failures on brand, thesis, audit and before/after at 1440, 768 and 390 px. Warnings left: Bone text reads as “cream” to the meter, there is no video, the brand board shows 12 type sizes because it reproduces other products’ layouts, and three typefaces, with mono only for machine strings.
axe-core, WCAG 2.2 AA
11 routes, night and day themes, 1440 and 390 px: 0 violations, after fixing one. The wallet’s sideways-scrolling tables weren’t keyboard-focusable on a phone.
Link previews
Every answer has its own page, so a shared link unfurls with that answer’s card. Checked on the live site in a preview renderer (opengraph.xyz): 0 errors. It caught a real problem: X lays the page title over the bottom left of the card, right on top of the proof line. The facts moved up and the bottom 160 px stays clear now.
Lighthouse
Accessibility 100 on all four pages measured. Best practices 100 on mobile and 96 on desktop. SEO 60 to 63, because the spec is set to noindex on purpose. Performance 98 to 100 on desktop (LCP 0.8 to 1.1 s). On mobile, Lighthouse’s simulated slow 4G gives 71 to 86 with LCP 3.7 to 6.8 s; with applied throttling the home page scores 93 (LCP 2.5 s) and the brand board 77 (TBT 0.75 s). The gap is JavaScript: about 218 KB unused, which server components would cut in the real build.

Tests with people

Built for at least five people outside our studio, comparing the live beta with the redesign. Each test runs on a phone or a laptop and gives back a block of answers to paste to us.

  • Five-second test →Both home screens, in random order, five seconds each. Then: what is it for, what would you do first, one word for how it felt.
  • First-click test →Two tasks on both versions: check a scam text, and find who answered and how sure they are. Records the first click and the time.
  • Blind image test →Your six Midjourney images mixed with four new ones. Mark the pictures that don’t belong.

Results so far: none. Not one of the five people per test has taken them yet.

Not done yet

Reported as plainly as what passed.

  • The five-second and first-click tests, and the blind image test, need at least five people outside our studio. The kits below are live; none has been run yet, so there are no results to report.
  • Mobile LCP is above 2.5 s in Lighthouse’s simulated profile on every page measured.
  • Two scope metrics land just outside your references’ range: shadow warmth (kept a little cool for night scenes) and midtone level (0.193 against a floor of 0.194).
  • Your Midjourney profile wasn’t available, so new images were generated in FLORA from your images and graded to match, not made in your exact house style.
  • Second opinion, changing the job type, scanning the link, keeping a job and registering a provider say what they would do; this spec replays only the four real jobs.
  • The link cards haven’t been posted on X, LinkedIn or Slack themselves yet; they were checked in a preview renderer (above).
  • Only the core flow and the wallet are designed. The other beta routes are mapped, not redrawn.
