What I found

Ranked by how much each one costs a first-time user.

  1. 1. 34 seconds of one static line

    The provider answered in about a second, but the job took 34 s, and for the first ~15 s the only feedback is “Working out what this question needs…”. The redesign shows each real stage as it happens.

  2. 2. The proof is hidden

    Provider, rank and the provider’s own confidence sit behind a collapsed “Where this came from” toggle. The confidence (0.70 on my first job) never appears at all.

  3. 3. “View signal” links to the wrong hash

    The Terminal links the receipt hash, which the explorer can’t find. The “How this was done” link uses the signal hash and works.

  4. 4. The public record leads with raw JSON

    The signal page cuts the input mid-word (“…payload”:{“query”:“Is”), files user jobs under category “other” with interest 0.0, and shows the JSON before any human summary.

  5. 5. An empty field sent to a provider

    On the “Hi mum” job RiskWire warned that the request’s question parameter arrived with length 0. It answered anyway, from the scenario text.

  6. 6. Explorer never finishes loading

    Network stats stay as skeletons and the leaderboard sits on “Loading leaderboard”. The integrations call it waits on returns 2.9 MB.

  7. 7. Mobile header and Terminal

    At 390 px the header shows two identical dashboard icons, and opening a past job puts an empty Terminal over the conversation.

  8. 8. Shared links show no picture

    Anjoman pages declare a small 500 × 500 link card, and its image URL points at anjoman.telegraphprotocol.com, which didn’t load when I checked. Telegraph’s own site already uses the large 1200 × 630 card.

  9. 9. The wording outran the confidence

    On the USPS job the provider reported 0.70 confidence; Anjoman’s answer said “highly likely” and showed no number. Wording should follow confidence.

  10. 10. The router can be confidently wrong

    In the public feed, “Will JAB-23E73 complete Phase 1 trials?” went to credit-score verification at 0.9 confidence. The redesign shows the job type it picked and lets you change it.

Your brand material

Your Drive folder, at full resolution.

Nine Midjourney images, one recipe. Six of them are above. Every filename carries --profile ar5id1l --v 8.2 and a one- or two-word prompt: “anjoman”, “anjoman enlightened”, “anjoman oracle”, “anjoman guru cult leader”. The look lives in the profile code, not the words. With access to that profile, new images come out in your exact house style; without it, I matched the look with a grade measured on all nine.

Anjoman Logo.pdf, 14 pages. A red hood with a dark arched face in a gold sun ring, then purple, black and navy variants, then dozens of turbans, seers and crystal balls. Many directions, no decision. One note on page 6 is the best idea in it: the face fills with light when your outcome is ready. The mark is built on that.

Telegraph. Hermes, the engraved winged messenger with cables; the Σ mark; the graded-diamond metaphor; MACHINA.
