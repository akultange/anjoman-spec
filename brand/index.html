One face for many minds

Anjoman means an assembly, a gathering where many minds meet. So the oracle has no face of its own. Where the face should be there is light, and the light is made by the assembly.

Built to work at 16 pixels

Two lancet arcs with their centres on the base line make the hood; a smaller lancet with a round foot makes the face. It sits on a 16 × 16 grid, so the straight edges land on whole pixels at 16, 32 and 64 px.

The hood is never solid white. Light comes from inside, so on dark grounds the one-colour mark is an outline with a lit face. That also keeps the silhouette reading as a cowl or an arch and nothing else.

In context

Checked where it actually lives: a browser tab beside other tabs, the circle X crops avatars to, and the app header.

Wordmark

Set in Markazi Text, a typeface built for Arabic script and Latin together by Borna Izadpanah, Florian Runge and Fiona Ross. Anjoman is a Persian word, so the name can be written in its own script without changing families.

Every lockup is an outlined vector, shaped from the font with its own kerning. Nothing here is a crop of the Midjourney art.

Colour

One grade for every image

Every new image goes through one grade, built the way a colourist builds a show look: balance each shot, apply one fixed look baked to a 33³ LUT, then add grain and halation. The targets come from scopes on your nine Midjourney references.

Waveform on the left of each, vectorscope on the right. The reference sits on a lifted floor with a narrow warm spike. The new image starts on the floor with a blue lobe; the grade lifts it and pulls the blue in.

Where imagery lives

The landing hero, the empty state, launch banners, share cards, the moments between jobs. Never behind an answer, a number or a receipt. The working screens stay quiet: night surfaces and one saffron light.

What the pictures avoid

No human face on the oracle, no one bowing to it, no crowd worshipping a leader. The figure faces you. Your Midjourney recipe calls it a “cult leader”; with a token in the picture, the public face should stay an oracle.
